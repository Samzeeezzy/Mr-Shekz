03 / ABOUT THE MINISTER

A LIFE OF
WORSHIP.

Gospel music minister · Singer · Music producer · Founder, Sheks Music World

Sheks JP, also known as Shekwolo Musa JP, is a Nigerian gospel music minister, singer, music producer and the founder of Sheks Music World. Based in Abuja, Nigeria, he has been active in music since 2020. His catalogue features gospel songs in Hausa and English, with themes of faith, thanksgiving, worship and the goodness of God. His releases are issued under the Sheks Music World name.
